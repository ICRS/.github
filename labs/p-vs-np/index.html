This is candidate-space growth, not a solver benchmark. It counts how many assignments exist, not how many steps any particular algorithm needs. The three-variable formula in the film is not being given more variables here — this is a schematic view of larger inputs.

The formula is fixed: (A ∨ B ∨ C) ∧ (¬A ∨ B ∨ ¬C) ∧ (A ∨ ¬B ∨ ¬C). This tiny instance teaches the mechanism — it is not evidence of hardness. General 3-SAT is NP-complete, but individual instances can be easy. A full check reads at most 3m literal occurrences, which is linear in the size of the encoded formula. The film illustrates the open question; it does not resolve it.
